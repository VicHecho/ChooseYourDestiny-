Hack The Matrix — 30 daily emails

Thirty early-morning emails for Choose Your Destiny, rotating through three kinds: a lesson, a code for the day, and a Dayplanner prompt. Each one is short enough to read before coffee.

Anything in gold is a spot for your own words from the book or your own code. I didn't have the book text, and I won't make up codes and pass them off as yours.

Setting this up in Shopify Email

  1. Shopify admin → Marketing → Create campaign → Shopify Email. Choose a text template.
  2. Copy in the subject, the preview text and the body for Day 1. Add your wordmark in the header block once, then save the whole thing as a template to reuse.
  3. Click Schedule and pick the date at 6:30 am ET. Repeat for each day. You can schedule all 30 ahead of time in one sitting.
  4. Send to Email subscribers. That list includes everyone who ticked the newsletter box on the intake form. Shopify adds the unsubscribe link and your address for you.
