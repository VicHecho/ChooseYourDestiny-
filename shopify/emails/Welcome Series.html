Welcome series

Four emails that go to every new subscriber over their first week: people who sign up through the wallpaper page, the newsletter box on the intake form, or your store footer.

It moves from who you are, to the Dayplanner, to the class, to booking a session. Spots marked in gold are for your own words.

Setting this up in Shopify

  1. Shopify admin → Marketing → Automations → Create automation. Choose the Welcome new subscribers template.
  2. Delete the discount step if the template includes one. Put in Email 1 below as the first email.
  3. Add a Wait step, then the next email. Use the delays listed on each card: send right away, then day 2, day 4 and day 7.
  4. Wait until the wallpaper page is live at /pages/wallpapers. Email 1's button points there. If you want to start sooner, delete that button and the line above it.
  5. Turn the automation on. From then on it runs by itself for every new subscriber.
  6. Hold the daily Hack The Matrix emails back until someone has finished this series, so their first week isn't a double dose.
